Full bio

Splittah is a Mulubinba (Newcastle) based DJ with ten years behind the decks and an all gas, no genre approach to the dancefloor. Known for high-energy selections and seamless blends, he moves fluidly through drum and bass, dubstep and 140, with the freedom to take a set wherever the room needs it to go.

From sweaty club nights to festival stages, Splittah has played venues including Ivy, Home the Venue and The Burdekin in Eora (Sydney), Argyle House and the Lass O’ Gowrie in Mulubinba (Newcastle), the Oxford in Wahluu (Bathurst), and The Grand in Dharawal Country (Wollongong). Along the way, he has appeared on lineups with crews including Bassaus, Steppers, Heaps Of, Courtyard, House of Traxx, Uno-Palooza, Rhythm at Dusk and Kicked Sundays, while sharing stages with artists including ShockOne, Calcium, Solomon France and Slumberjack.

A career highlight came with a set at Fiji’s Your Paradise festival. Whether it is a club, festival or late-night party, the approach stays the same: tight selections, serious energy and a willingness to take the set wherever the room wants to go.
