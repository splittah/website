02 / Bio

Artist Bio

Copy, paste, job done.

Approved bio

Splittah is a Mulubinba (Newcastle) based DJ with ten years behind the decks and an all gas, no genre approach to the dancefloor. Moving through drum and bass, dubstep, 140 and whatever else the room calls for, his sets are built around high-energy selections, seamless blends and reading the crowd rather than staying locked into one lane.

From sweaty club nights to festival stages, he has played across Australia and internationally, sharing stages with artists including ShockOne, Calcium, Solomon France and Slumberjack, with a career highlight at Fiji’s Your Paradise festival. Whatever the setting, the goal stays simple: keep the room moving.
