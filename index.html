About

Splittah is a Mulubinba-based DJ with nine years behind the decks and a reputation for bringing an all gas, no genre approach to the dancefloor. Known for his high-energy selections and seamless blends, he moves fluidly through drum and bass, 140, UKG, house, and techno—always reading the room and delivering exactly what the moment calls for.

From sweaty club nights to festival stages, Splittah has brought his sound to iconic venues like Ivy, Home the Venue and The Burdekin in Eora (Sydney), Argyle House and the Lass O’ Gowrie in Mulubinba (Newcastle), the Oxford in Wahluu (Bathurst), and The Grand in Dharawal Country (Wollongong). Along the way, he’s jumped on lineups with crews including Bassaus, Steppers, Heaps Of, Courtyard, House of Traxx, Uno-Palooza, Rhythm at Dusk and Kicked Sundays, while sharing stages with artists such as ShockOne, Calcium, Solomon France, and Slumberjack.

A career highlight came with his set at Fiji’s Your Paradise festival, proving his ability to light up both intimate dancefloors and large-scale events. No matter the setting, Splittah’s focus remains the same: tight selections, serious energy, and keeping the crowd locked in from the first drop to the very last beat.
